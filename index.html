Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

Learn the basics and enjoy the game!

Basic Rules

  • Each team has 6 players.
  • Teams can touch the ball 3 times.
  • Do not catch or hold the ball.
  • Send the ball over the net.
  • A set is usually played to 25 points.

Equipment

  • Volleyball
  • Net and court
  • Sports jersey
  • Knee pads
  • Volleyball shoes

Basic Skills

  • Serving
  • Passing
  • Setting
  • Spiking
  • Blocking

Why Play Volleyball?

Volleyball helps improve teamwork, communication, and coordination. It is also a fun way to stay active and make friends.

Practice, teamwork, and have fun! 🏐
